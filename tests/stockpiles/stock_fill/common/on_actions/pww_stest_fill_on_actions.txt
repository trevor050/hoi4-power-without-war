#              quota's request through the cost modifier). Every 5 days to
#              day 61: resource@tungsten stays >= 0, output is not reduced

#              exports never fall below the day-1 figure by more than 2, the
#              stock grows past 100.
#   usa_fill   USA steel: its free-trade export pool is fully sold; it still
#              stores its leftover and keeps its exports.
#   decay      Iceland (ICE) neither makes nor uses tungsten: 1000 units set
#              on day 1 lose about 2% a month and nothing else (day 61).
#   value      day 62/64 on POR: pww_stock_value_steel falls with fill (0,
#              50, 100%) and rises with world tension (+60 named threat).
