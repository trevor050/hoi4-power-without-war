#   C desperate      GER sells 16 steel to POR above market (3 factories):

#                    one ends its sibling.
#   G market_line    the market verdict the screen shows agrees with the

#   H lease_mines    Lease Mines opens on a lease of one of POR's producing

#   I balance_it     "Balance it" brings below-market, above-market,
#                    empty-side and political-power-only drafts to market

#                    screen shows (rows, totals) are the acceptance model's:
#                    terms computed from the shown rows (times the target's
#                    appetite) equal the terms.
#   J appetite       the same political-power-only draft at market: once POR
