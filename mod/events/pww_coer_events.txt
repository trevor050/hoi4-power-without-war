# recipient first (country vars pww_coer_ev_*, readout pww_coer_rd_*).

#   pww_coer.3  claimant: the target is Cornered
#   pww_coer.4  claimant: a quiet demand has leaked
#   pww_coer.5  claimant: the demand is resolved (no situation attached)
#   pww_coer.6  target: the demand is resolved (no situation attached)
#   pww_coer.7  third party: asked to join an embargo (FROM claimant)
#   pww_coer.8  target: strangulation grants a war justification
###############################################################################
